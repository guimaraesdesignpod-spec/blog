# About Brainwire

**Brainwire** is where the business of AI gets decoded — no corporate speak, no venture-capital hype, just honest analysis from people who actually use these tools every day.

## What We Cover

We track the AI tools, trends, and market shifts that actually matter if you're a developer, content creator, freelancer, or small business owner trying to work smarter. Not the press releases. Not the valuation rounds. The stuff that changes how you work.

- **Tool comparisons** with real prices and honest pros/cons
- **Industry analysis** on what Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are actually doing
- **Practical workflows** — automating the boring parts with AI
- **Strategy** — how to position yourself as the tools evolve

## Why "Brainwire"?

AI is rewiring how work happens. Brainwire is the signal running through that change — focused, practical, and no-nonsense.

## Who's Behind This

I'm **Elisio Oliveira**, a developer and automation nerd based in Portugal. I build AI pipelines that generate content, automate research, and orchestrate workflows — and I write about what I learn in the process.

Brainwire started as my personal lab notes. It turned into something worth sharing.
